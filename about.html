I’m Markus Pfeifer, born in 2002 and based in Graz, Austria. In 2026, I graduated from the Ortweinschule, where I studied graphic and communication design in the evening college.

Typography and art direction are what draw me in the most. I like to work on the details of a letter, the rhythm of a page and the idea that holds it all together. I’m never satisfied with the first solution that comes to mind; I keep looking for the one that is more thoughtful, more surprising and more my own.
