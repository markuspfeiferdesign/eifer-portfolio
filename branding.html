UMADUM FILMFEST

Out of the Cinema, Into the Alps

Branding & Visual Identity. Concept project created during my studies at Ortweinschule Graz.

The »Umadum Filmfest« is a film festival on the move. Instead of one fixed venue, it screens films at Austria’s most remarkable natural and cultural locations, from alpine lakes to hilltop fortresses. Every evening becomes a small adventure.

The visual identity combines a vintage alpine look with the spirit of a summer open-air cinema. Sunburst rays, a bold three-dimensional logotype and a warm palette of burnt orange, deep brown and cream recall classic travel posters and old cinema signage. Atmospheric landscape photography keeps the focus on the places themselves.

The campaign line »Rein Raus ins Kino« plays with the German phrase for going into the cinema. By striking out »Rein«, the festival invites its audience to go out instead.

The system runs across posters, outdoor advertising, social media, tickets, merchandise and web, all built on one flexible, recognisable look.
